# Some great final projects

Source: https://manipulation.csail.mit.edu/misc.html#projects (retrieved 2026-10-27). Extracted from the page; the text and links are the course site's.


Each semester students put together a final project using the tools from this class. So many of them are fantastic! Here is a small sample that captures some of the diversity.

**Fall 2023 Outstanding Project Awards:**

- [OrderBot: Food Preparation Manipulation Bot for Fast Food Environments](https://youtu.be/zOvT7VnuZ2k) by Shreya Chaudhary,
- [APPLE-E: A Compliant Apple Picking Robot](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hq2LtTeIHc) by Udayan Bulchandani,
- [Guitar Hero Bot: Motion Planning to Solve Rhythm Games with Robotic Manipulation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dVkg_O926Yw) by Ryan Hourican, John Readlinger, and Noah Fisher,
- [An Autonomous Pool-Playing Robot](https://youtu.be/578RsYdkPGs) by Jinger Chong and Eugenia Feng,
- [Dreidel Spinning Bot: Exploring Contact Models in Simulation](https://youtu.be/0svE_b9JE_E) by Lily Yeazell,
- [The Robot Librarian: Mobile Manipulation in a Library](https://youtu.be/U6YdzsNt3tA) by Shiva Sreeram and Peter Holderrieth,
- [CatchingBot: A Robotic System for Catching Objects in Flight](https://youtu.be/TrhjG72PJNU) (including online estimation/replanning) by Bartłomiej Cieslar and Michael Zeng,
- [ShelveBot: Fast Pick-and-Place in Human Form Factors](https://youtu.be/Mf1hoPnk0ik) by Julianna Schneider and Shayan Pardis and Anna Yang.
